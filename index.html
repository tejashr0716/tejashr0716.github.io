03 — Selected work

Featured projects

Each project exists to hold one invariant under pressure. Design docs, ADRs and load-test results live in every repo.

Project / 001

Surge

In progress

A flash-sale checkout engine built for the worst case: 1,000 units of inventory and 50,000 buyers arriving in three seconds. It holds four invariants — zero oversell, no double charge, every order reaches a terminal state, and the system degrades instead of collapsing.

Inventory is reserved through an atomic Redis Lua script, payments are made idempotent with stored-response replay, and failures unwind through saga compensations backed by a transactional outbox and a replayable dead-letter queue.

Project / 002

Fleet

In progress

A real-time vehicle tracking platform: ingest thousands of GPS pings per second, answer "nearest five vehicles" in under 50 ms, and stream live positions to subscribers over WebSockets.

Positions are bucketed with H3 spatial indexing so proximity queries never scan the fleet, hot city cells are handled without stalling ingest, and slow subscribers get backpressure and dropped stale frames rather than unbounded buffers.

Project / 003

Halt

In progress

A traffic-violation detection pipeline that stays healthy when inference is slower than ingest. Frames flow through a sampling stage into a pool of inference workers, with per-stage queue lag exposed as metrics.

The same vehicle is deduplicated across consecutive frames by plate hash and time window, and record generation is idempotent — replaying a stream produces byte-identical output instead of duplicate violations.

Project / 004

Tracekit

Shipped

A GPS map-matching engine that snaps noisy traces onto an OpenStreetMap road graph using a Hidden Markov Model with Viterbi decoding, reaching 90%+ segment accuracy.

Per-trace matching came down from seconds to under 200 ms by replacing full-table candidate scans with spatial indexing and a bounded candidate radius, served as a FastAPI service with a Leaflet tool for overlaying raw versus matched trajectories.
